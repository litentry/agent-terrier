# Nutrition basics the plan leans on

General reference for a household planner — NOT medical advice; a bound gene
report or a doctor's instruction in the preferences resource always wins.

- Protein: roughly 0.8 g per kg of body weight per day for adults; spread over
  meals rather than one large portion.
- Vegetables: aim for five servings a day (a serving ≈ one fist); vary colors
  across the week.
- Staples to rotate: grains, legumes, eggs, fish, poultry, tofu — one protein
  per meal is plenty.
- Kids and elders: smaller portions, softer textures, less salt; never skip
  breakfast.
- Leftovers: reuse within two days; anything with a sour smell or slime is
  `spoiling`, not "probably fine".
